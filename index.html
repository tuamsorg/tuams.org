PSBT

Why?

Tuams introduces a new dimension in Bitcoin security: Geography.

French Polynesia has one of the largest sets of atolls in the world: the Tuamotu — nicknamed Tuams.

Generation

A Trezor Safe 5 (Seedless, MicroSD Card Encryption) is deposited on an atoll.

The MicroSD needed to use it is kept in Tahiti.

Opening

  1. 1Select an atoll on the map and pick an available xpub.
  2. 2Pay the opening fees with a Bitcoin address that you own.
  3. ⚷︎Now your payment address is your PROOF address linked to the selected xpub.

Integration

  1. 3Integrate the xpub into the architecture of your choice.
  2. ⚠︎Plan for an independent recovery method.

Signing

  1. 4Send your PSBT → PSBT@tuams.org.
  2. 5Pay the travel fees with your PROOF Bitcoin address.
  3. ✈︎Tuams is leaving Tahiti for the atoll to sign and return your PSBT.
  4. 6You can broadcast it.
