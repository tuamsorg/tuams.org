Why?

Tuams introduces a new dimension in Bitcoin security: Geography.

French Polynesia has one of the largest sets of atolls in the world: the Tuamotu — nicknamed Tuams.

Generation

A Trezor Safe 5 (Seedless, MicroSD Card Encryption) is deposited on an atoll.

The MicroSD needed to use it is kept in Tahiti.

Opening

  • 1Select an available atoll on the map.
  • 2Pay the opening fees with a Bitcoin address that you own.
  • ⚷︎Now your sending address becomes your PROOF address.

Integration

  • 3Integrate the xpub into the architecture of your choice.
  • ⚠︎Plan for an independent recovery method.

Signing

  • 4Send your PSBT → PSBT@tuams.org [PGP].
  • 5Pay the travel fees with your PROOF Bitcoin address.
  • ✈︎Tuams is leaving Tahiti for the atoll to sign and return your PSBT.
  • 6You can sign and broadcast it.
